Episode one was a history lecture. Episode two is a guy telling me college might be a scam while I'm literally paying tuition. Rude. Also correct-ish.

**How late am I? ⏰** The original dropped February 6, 2019. I was in seventh grade, failing at Fortnite builds. Late by 7 years, 8 months and 3 weeks. The remaster landed in August, so only two months behind on that one.

## Who is John Carvalho, according to my ears

Vlad introduces him as "the Bitcoin error log" and basically a lone wolf who doesn't try to fit into a mold. Then Vlad's first question is how he got into Bitcoin, and John's answer is the most 2011 sentence ever: he kept seeing articles about people ordering drugs online with "this online currency." Forums, Reddit, IRC. Mt. Gox. The full starter pack.

## The parts that cooked me

- **The Bitcoin Foundation got roasted.** John's take is that those early organizations mostly served as "a platform for the egos of the early businessmen of Bitcoin."
- **"Everybody who ever sold a Bitcoin was wrong."** He says he tweeted this around the 2017 peak, then immediately admits he sold too and isn't rich because of it. Early doesn't automatically mean loaded. Humbling.
- **Bitcoins want to be free.** His "abstract theory": holding coins is like trying to hold the One Ring. "It has a toxic effect and eventually you have to let them go." Lord of the Rings as a financial thesis was not on my bingo card.
- **The Roger Ver lore.** Vlad asks if the infamous interview was scripted. John says no, he prepared a lot, watched Roger's videos, and said "Bitcoin Cash" properly until "Bcash" slipped out by habit. Then, once it was clearly going nowhere, he admits: "So I just did a little Bcash dance."

**Lore Drop 📜:** Near the end Vlad literally spells out "C-A-N-T-I-L-L-O-N" so listeners can look up the Cantillon effect, the idea that freshly printed money reaches banks and elites before it reaches regular people's paychecks. John closes with the line "you need a Bitcoin to come around every thousand years to fix it," and I wrote it on a sticky note.

## Side Quest 🎓

The part that got me was John's backstory: wanted to be a comic book artist, played in a punk band, started a record label, did one semester at UMass and quit because "I felt like I was paying to go to high school."

I made the mistake of quoting this in the dorm at 1 a.m. Dante immediately announced he's dropping out to trade full-time. I asked with what money. He said "my memecoins want to be free." Bro used the One Ring theory against me. We argued until 2:30. Neither of us is dropping out.

## Worth it?

It's almost two hours and the middle wanders into price talk, but the Foundation stories and the Roger Ver behind-the-mic confessions are pure lore. The show is apparently [remastering the whole early catalog](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/remastering-bitcoin-takeover-podcast-2026-2027-project/), which is great news for latecomers like me. Also, he's been back a bunch of times since, so the [rest of his appearances](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/tag/john-carvalho/) are now on my list.
