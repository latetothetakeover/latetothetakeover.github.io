Three posts in, I did the responsible thing and went back to the very first episode. Found the show at episode 360-something, now watching the pilot. Classic me.

**How late am I? ⏰** The original aired February 2, 2019. I was 13 and my whole personality was Fortnite. Late by 7 years, 8 months and 3 weeks. The remaster came out in August, so at least that one I'm only two months late on.

## The cold open hits

Vlad opens by saying you're listening to the first ever remastered episode, and that Donald McIntyre passed in 2025. So you go in knowing you're hearing someone who isn't around anymore. Heavy start. Then he warns you that his accent "used to be much weirder than it is right now," and he's not lying, and I love him for saying it first.

Early on they both get stuck on the word for money that can't be confiscated. "Inconfiscable"? Vlad: "we're both not English native speakers." Two guys inventing vocabulary on episode one. Peak origin story.

## The ideas that broke my brain

- **Our brains are built for small villages.** Donald's take: humans spent millions of years in small groups, and only the last 10,000 or so in giant cities and nations. Bitcoin, in his view, is the cypherpunks pushing power back toward the individual. Never thought of Bitcoin as an anthropology thing.
- **Reading Nick Szabo is a workout.** Donald says he has to read Szabo's articles about 30 times, because "one article is like six months of university courses." As someone paying for actual university courses: offended.
- **Money might be ancient. Like, ancient ancient.** He talks about prehistoric stashes of stone hand axes, sometimes up to 200, piled up and never used, like a vault. His speculation: "Maybe money is 3 million years old." I texted that to Dante. He replied "so my memecoins are basically rocks." Yes, buddy.
- **The privacy irony.** Mid-interview Donald points out that they're talking over Zoom and "we don't know if it's being recorded somewhere." Bro, it was. I'm listening to it in 2026.

**Lore Drop 📜:** Donald says he actually interacted with Tim May, the cypherpunk legend who had passed the month before, and that May held stocks and gold but no Bitcoin, because he didn't need to move money across borders. A cypherpunk OG with zero sats. Also, the show apparently has [five official rules](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/the-5-rules-of-the-bitcoin-takeover-podcast/), and now I need to know if episode one followed them.

## Side Quest 🍗

Last Sunday I went home to Marietta for laundry and Mom's baked chicken. I tried the "money is 3 million years old" fact at the table. Dad, the cash-and-invoices guy, said "so the caveman also didn't trust the bank." Jade just said "the little internet coin is a rock now?" Eli asked if he could have the rest of my chicken. Dinner was a W overall.

## The spicy ending

Near the end Donald drops a criticism of Bitcoin I genuinely hadn't heard: that "the 21 million monetary policy is a political decision," backed by agreement rather than enforced by proof of work. Not qualified to judge it. It still kept me up.

Also Vlad closes with a pun about the talk being "enlightening" because of the Lightning Network. Seven years later, the dad jokes are clearly part of the brand.

If you're new like me, [the remastered S1 E1 with Donald McIntyre](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/remembering-donald-mcintyre-s1-e1-remaster-on-bitcoin-tim-may-nick-szabo-and-the-history-of-money/) is two hours of a polymath thinking out loud.

**Rating: ★★★★½ (4.5/5).** Huge lore value, a little long in the Ethereum Classic stretch, and lowkey moving knowing what you know going in. Top 10 shortlist, no cap.
