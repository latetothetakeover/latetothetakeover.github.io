Day two of a conference I didn't attend, watched from a library two miles away, reviewed by a guy who owns exactly one phone wallet. We are so back.

[Part 2 of Bitcoin Takeover's TABConf 8 livestream](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/s17-e47-tabconf-8-livestream-with-kevin-loaec-ekrem-bal-spaces-protocol-the-bitcoin-pivot/) is the same setup as Part 1: tripod, camera, a stack of Forks! magazines, and whoever walks past the table. This time the WiFi fought back and split it in two.

**How late am I? ⏰** Posted October 16, 2026. Late by five days.

## Kevin Loaec ended my whole career

Kevin runs Wizardsardine, the company behind the Liana wallet, and Liana exists so that one hacked or buggy device doesn't wreck you: multisig plus timelocked recovery keys. Then he says the line: "A lot of people are still using single sig. And they're like, why? It's 2026."

Sir. I am people. I felt that one in my spine.

He didn't sugarcoat anything. On vaults: "We know vaults don't really have demand, no matter what people say." On the Coldcard random number bug, which he was involved in disclosing, it was "very, very shocking that this bug was there for five years." And on funding open source: "I don't want to be begging to my users." Respect.

## Citrea, Crest and a $10 AI allowance

Ekrem Bal from Citrea opened the stream by saying they're "pivoting to privacy" with an app called Crest. His reasoning was about safety, not hiding: there are "physical attacks happening all the time around the world." The detail I can't stop thinking about: he gave his own AI agent "a spending limit of ten dollars per day," with a hardware wallet as the boss. More discipline than my whole friend group.

## Spaces: usernames that live forever

Andrew and Buffer from Spaces Protocol explained names on Bitcoin where "the only thing that gets put on Bitcoin is the 32 bytes hash." Andrew registered @tabconf, gave attendees free handles, then sealed it so "no other handle could be issued at the tabconf namespace." A souvenir from the last TABConf that can never be minted again, and I didn't get one. Classic me.

Also, Andrew warned people not to send money to the address tied to their name, because a famous permanent address raises "your kind of vulnerability for wrench attack." Priya would've stood up and clapped.

**Lore Drop 📜:** Namecoin, the first altcoin, drowned in name squatters. Spaces avoids that with only 10 names entering auction per day and 10-day auctions where late bids extend the deadline.

## Side Quest 🎉

Last weekend Marcus dragged me to a house party, and someone asked why I'm so protective of my phone. I said "my single-sig wallet" out loud. Silence. A girl near the snacks said "is that a dating app?" Marcus will be bringing this up until graduation. Lesson: never say "single sig" at a party.

**Explaining it to Uncle Ray:** Liana is like keeping a spare boat key at your brother's house that only works if you haven't touched the boat in six months.
