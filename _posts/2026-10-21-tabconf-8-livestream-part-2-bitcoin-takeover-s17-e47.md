Day two of a conference I didn't attend, watched from a library two miles away, reviewed by a guy who owns exactly one phone wallet. We are so back.

[Part 2 of Bitcoin Takeover's TABConf 8 livestream](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/s17-e47-tabconf-8-livestream-with-kevin-loaec-ekrem-bal-spaces-protocol-the-bitcoin-pivot/) is the same picture as day one: two wooden armchairs, a giant gold "eCash.com" banner, and Vlad pointing a handheld mic at whoever sits down. Half the guests end up flipping through his Forks! magazine on camera. Two minutes in, he casually mentions he "destroyed the computer" for Citrea's launch ceremony. Normal day.

**How late am I? ⏰** Posted October 16, 2026. Late by five days.

## Kevin Loaec ended my whole career

Kevin (baseball cap) makes the Liana wallet, which exists so one hacked or buggy device doesn't wreck you: multisig plus timelocked recovery keys. Then he says it: "A lot of people are still using single SIG and they're like, why? It's 2026."

Sir. I am people. I felt that one in my spine.

Zero sugarcoating. On vaults: "We know vaults don't really have demand." On the Coldcard random number bug: "very very shocking that this bug was there for five years." On funding open source: "I don't want to be begging to my users." He even posts a job ad mid-stream for a head of sales because "I'm terrible at it." Honest king.

Later, after he and Vlad discover matching Citizen watches (orange vs "indigo blue"), Kevin drops the line I wrote on my hand: "a bunch of nerds just completely reset the system." Vlad instantly: "we haven't reset anything." Peak podcast.

## Citrea, Crest and a $10 AI allowance

Ekrem Bal opens the stream (Vlad calls him CEO, gets corrected live: chief scientist). Citrea is going into privacy with a wallet called Crest, and his reason is safety, not hiding: "physical attacks happening all the time around the world." Also, he gave his AI agent a "spending limit of $10 per day," with his hardware wallet as the authorizer. More discipline than my friend group.

## Spaces: usernames that live forever

Andrew and Buffer from Spaces Protocol explain names on Bitcoin where "the only thing that gets put on Bitcoin is the 32 bytes hash." Andrew gave TABConf attendees free @tabconf handles with a coupon code, and the plan is to burn the namespace afterward so no new @tabconf name can ever exist. A last-TABConf souvenir, and I missed it. Classic me.

Andrew also warns that a famous address tied to your name raises "your kind of vulnerability for wrench attack." Priya would've clapped.

**Lore Drop 📜:** Namecoin, the first altcoin, drowned in squatters. Spaces only lets 10 names per day into the active auction, so nobody can land-grab everything overnight.

## The sandwich arc

Vlad keeps asking if there are still sandwiches down the hall. Final answer: "They're gone." After Part 1's $10,000 sandwich lore, TABConf lunch stays undefeated.

Last weekend Marcus dragged me to a house party, and someone asked why I'm so protective of my phone. I said "my single-sig wallet" out loud. Silence. A girl by the snacks asked if that's a dating app.
