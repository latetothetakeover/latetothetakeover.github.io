Two posts ago I watched Michael Tidwell show up at his own conference's funeral. So obviously I had to go find out who the funeral guy actually is. Turns out he's the guy who solves Bitcoin drama with a carnival wheel.

**How late am I? ⏰** Aired September 9, 2025, about a year before the last TABConf. I was a freshman figuring out the dining hall. Late by 1 year, 1 month and 2 weeks.

## The format is unhinged (affectionately)

This one's a livestream with [Michael Tidwell, the TABConf organizer](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/tag/michael-tidwell/), and the gimmick is perfect. Behind Michael there's a spinning wheel of who's to blame. On Vlad's side there's a bowl of plastic eggs with folded paper "solutions" inside. Problem, spin, egg. Science.

Michael's reaction to being asked to trust the randomness: "Do we even need cryptography? I mean, come on. We have all this trust going on."

## The spins that ate

- **Bitcoin Core vs Knots.** The wheel says: the plebs. The egg says: "use tabs." Vlad explains that it's an old Adam Back answer about avoiding high fees: don't settle every drink on the base layer, run a tab like at a bar. Fully random. Somehow made sense.
- **What even is a pleb?** Michael: "I don't even think plebs are real. I think they're like birds." I laughed out loud in the library and got a look.
- **Why no drivechains yet?** The wheel lands on spooks: "Paul is up against the spooks." The egg: hard fork. Michael goes "Dude, this might actually happen." Reader, I have seen the episode list. Paul Sztorc literally forked Bitcoin to launch eCash a few months later. The eggs know things.
- **Lightning not living up to the hype?** Jack Dorsey, one slot away from Blockstream. The chat picks the egg, and it's "Listen to the Bitcoin Takeover podcast." Vlad admits: "I had to put the shameless plug inside." Respect.
- **Craig Wright?** Wheel says Roger Ver, egg says "Sell all your Bitcoin." Do not take life advice from eggs.

Also, Michael's kid wanders into frame at one point, and Vlad notes it's the first time a kid has shown his face on the show. Wholesome jumpscare.

## The part that actually got me

Near the end Vlad asks where newbies should even go for clarity. Michael's answer: "you should always assume Bitcoin was a terrible idea and you should prove to yourself otherwise." Then he adds, okay, come to TABConf either way. That's basically this blog's mission statement, minus the conference, which I missed. Twice now, emotionally.

**Lore Drop 📜:** Michael calls Aaron van Wirdum's *The Genesis Book* "my favorite book hands down" among Bitcoin books. Added to the list. There's an episode with Aaron in the archives too, so that's coming.

## Side Quest 🎣

Saturday I went fishing with Uncle Ray on Lake Lanier, first time since summer. Two hours, zero bites. I tried to explain the wheel of blame and he said that's how he and Mom split the bill at Thanksgiving. Then he caught a bass while I was mid-sentence about Knots. He didn't say anything. He didn't have to.

**Explaining it to Uncle Ray:** Bitcoin drama is two guys arguing about the right knot while the fish swims off.

Watching this a year before the [last TABConf](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/tag/tabconf/) hits different, though.

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5).** Long, chaotic, more fun than half my lectures. Future Top 10 contender for format alone.
