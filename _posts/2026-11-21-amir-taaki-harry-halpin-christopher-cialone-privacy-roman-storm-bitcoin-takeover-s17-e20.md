Nopara made privacy sound like math. This episode made it sound like a fight. Privacy arc, level three.

**How late am I? ⏰** Aired April 24, 2026. I was a freshman cramming for finals with zero idea this show existed. Late by just under seven months, basically live by my standards.

## The cold open is a knife

First thing on screen: Amir Taaki, lead dev of DarkFi, flipping a knife while Vlad introduces him. Then Harry Halpin, CEO of Nym, and Christopher Cialone, who's been following the Roman Storm case up close. The vibe never settles.

Harry goes first because he has to leave early, and he doesn't waste it. A fired-up rant about Palantir's CEO Alex Karp and his book *The Technological Republic*. His sharpest point, to me, is the business one: government contracts with a 90% renewal rate, paying again and again for software that "effectively is probably database joins." Econ major brain lit up. That's a subscription you can't cancel.

The line I wrote down: **"the internet does not belong to a single country."**

Highlights that stuck:

- **Christopher on the Tornado Cash trial.** He says he sat in on the Rule 29 hearing, and that the prosecutor argued even legitimate users' funds were a problem because they made the pools more private. His take: Roman "didn't go out of his way to stick it to the feds."
- **The VPN segment.** Basically: if a VPN wants your email and signup info, that's a red flag, because they already know your real IP. The ones they like let you pay in Bitcoin, Monero or Zcash and just hand you an account number. Browser verdict: Firefox and Brave. Christopher's homework: build your own cyberdeck and become "an all-around technologist."
- **Amir's travel lore.** Vlad brings up the Argentina story and Amir confirms it on air: "They deported me from Argentina to Brazil. And uh they wouldn't tell me why." Then he lists detentions in Serbia and Mexico like it's a layover schedule.
- **The chaos.** Amir's phone keeps dropping, the Zcash giveaway turns into a number-guessing game with averages, and when Amir shows off his Lara Croft stuff, someone says it feels like a privacy talk but "we're actually on MTV Cribs with Amir." Accurate.

Fair warning: it gets political. Iran, ICE, the president. Not refereeing that.

**Lore Drop 📜:** Christopher got into Bitcoin in 2012 as a private investigator who couldn't code an HTML page. He heard Charlie Shrem speak, went to a Bitcoin storefront on Wall Street, and says it took him about three weeks just to figure out how to buy any. I complained when my app took three days.

## Side Quest 🔧

Last Saturday I rode along with Dad on a furnace call, because November in Georgia means every heater that sat quiet all summer suddenly dies. On the drive he mentioned his insurance offered a discount if he put a driving-tracker app on the work van. He said no. "Then they know every house I go to." I nearly dropped the toolbox. Dad accidentally summed up the episode better than I could.

**Explaining it to Uncle Ray:** a VPN that wants your name is like a fishing guide who writes down your secret spot "for safety."
