Last week Amir Taaki flipped a knife on a 2026 livestream. This week: the origin story. Spoiler, it's 2010 Bitcoin.

**How late am I? ⏰** Aired September 28, 2022. I was 16, a high-school junior whose entire financial plan was birthday money in a sock drawer. Late by 4 years and 2 months.

## The guy who made Bitcoin write things down

A 43-minute one-on-one about the era I keep calling the lore I missed. Vlad opens by listing what Amir built: the BIP standard, libbitcoin (the first alternative implementation of Bitcoin) and Dark Wallet, an early attempt at a privacy wallet.

Amir says he showed up in late 2010 wanting to build peer-to-peer poker software, because he was a professional poker player. Then Bitcoin itself got more interesting than the poker. Relatable.

What stuck:

- **Four tribes, one coin.** Early Bitcoin pulled together free-software hackers, Austrian-economics "gold people", crypto-anarchist cypherpunks and agorists. Explains a LOT about Bitcoin Twitter.
- **The Gavin DM.** Amir says that after what he calls the first presentation about Bitcoin, at a bankers' conference in Amsterdam, Gavin Andresen messaged him on IRC: "I saw your talk, I think you shouldn't talk about Bitcoin in public again." His reply, censored for my mom: "dude, who the [bleep] are you telling me what to do."
- **Privacy receipts.** He says he wrote the first implementation of CoinJoin and of stealth addresses. Me, two posts after learning what a CoinJoin even is: humbled.
- **The money lecture.** Out of nowhere he goes to ancient Mesopotamia: clay tokens standing for grain or livestock in storehouses, with actual metal money only showing up much later. His conclusion: "the financial networks are more primary than the money itself." My econ professor owes him a guest-lecture fee.

The line I wrote down: **"Bitcoin is not for buying a coffee."**

## The part that's kind of sad

He says he "never kept any Bitcoins," reinvesting everything into the work. By the end he sounds disillusioned: lots of good minds left, and anytime someone proposes something new the answer is "no, Bitcoin's perfect as it is." I've been here six weeks, so I can't judge. But it's the opposite of number-go-up energy, and that's why it hits.

**Lore Drop 📜:** BIPs, the numbered proposals every upgrade fight revolves around, were born from a fight. Amir says the other side was rushing changes into the code, so his side decided "we need to slow this stuff down." Today's "Bitcoin should never change" conservatism, he says, has its roots right there.

## Side Quest 🦃

Thanksgiving at my parents' place. After dinner Jade slapped masking tape on the best leftover container: "JADE. MAC & CHEESE. DO NOT TOUCH." Eli answered with a sticky note on the pecan pie. By 9 PM the fridge was a full claims system, with Dad settling disputes like a central bank. I told everyone this was literally Mesopotamian clay tokens. Jade said, "Nobody asked, Nico." Then Eli traded me half his pie claim for my turkey leg. Markets.

**Explaining it to Uncle Ray:** BIPs are the lake's no-wake rules. Slow, annoying, and the reason nobody's boat gets flipped.
