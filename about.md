Hey, I'm Nico: 20, econ major in Atlanta, recovering "Bitcoin is just a stock, right?" guy. I found the [Bitcoin Takeover podcast](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/) at TABConf 8, the final one, watching its livestream from the conference, so yeah, I'm late. Like, 360-episodes late. Now I'm speedrunning the whole back catalog, one episode every couple of days. These are my personal takeaways, ratings and the lore I can't stop thinking about.

Every post ends with a rating out of 5 stars. Once I hit 20 reviews I'll start a running Top 10.

I'm pseudonymous on purpose: no face pics, no last name, no wallet addresses. Privacy is kind of the point.

**This is an unofficial fan blog. I'm not affiliated with Bitcoin Takeover or Vlad Costea, and nothing here is financial advice.** Go listen to the real thing at [bitcoin-takeover.com](https://bitcoin-takeover.com/).
